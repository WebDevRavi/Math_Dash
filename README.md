# MathDash

> **Think Fast. Calculate Faster.**

MathDash is a fast-paced mental arithmetic game built around quick thinking, rapid decision-making, and high-score chasing.

The goal is simple: **solve as many math questions as possible before the timer runs out.**

---

## 🎮 Features

- ⚡ Fast-paced mental arithmetic gameplay
- 🧠 Multiple difficulty modes
- 🎯 Two-answer choice system
- 🔥 Streak-based gameplay
- ⭐ Performance/star system
- ⏱️ 60-second gameplay sessions
- ➕ Time Bonus power-up
- 👋 First-time player guidance
- 🔊 Background and gameplay audio
- 📱 Responsive gameplay
- 🌐 Browser-based gameplay
- 🎮 CrazyGames SDK integration

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Choose your difficulty mode.
2. A mathematical question appears.
3. Choose the correct answer from the two options.
4. Correct answers increase your score and streak.
5. Build a streak of fast correct answers to earn the **Time Bonus**.
6. Use the Time Bonus to gain additional time.
7. Keep solving questions until the timer reaches zero.
8. Try to beat your high score!

---

## 🎚️ Difficulty Modes

MathDash includes four difficulty modes:

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🟢 **Easy** | Simple calculations for beginners |
| 🔵 **Normal** | Balanced difficulty |
| 🟠 **Hard** | More challenging calculations |
| 🔴 **Very Hard** | High-intensity mode for experienced players |

The currently selected difficulty is displayed during gameplay so players always know which mode they are playing.

---

## ⚡ Time Bonus

Build a streak of **3 fast consecutive correct answers** to earn the Time Bonus.

Once earned, the player can activate the bonus to receive additional time.

The first-time player guide introduces this mechanic naturally during gameplay.

---

## 🔥 Streak System

Correct consecutive answers build your streak.

Maintaining a streak rewards fast and accurate gameplay, while an incorrect answer resets the streak according to the game's rules.

The goal is to stay focused and keep the streak alive.

---

## ⭐ Performance System

MathDash includes a **five-star performance system** that reflects how well the player performs during a game.

Play faster, answer accurately, and aim for a perfect performance.

---

## 👋 First-Time Player Guide

MathDash includes a lightweight onboarding system designed to teach the game without interrupting the experience.

A simple animated hand guides new players through:

- Selecting an answer
- Building a streak
- Earning the Time Bonus
- Activating the Time Bonus

The tutorial is designed to appear during the first gameplay experience and stay out of the way for returning players.
